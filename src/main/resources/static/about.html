Why this matters

Banks increasingly run on cloud providers, payment processors and technology vendors, and those vendors run on their own subcontractors. When one link fails, the bank is still accountable to its customers and its regulator.

30%
of confirmed data breaches involved a third party, double the 15% reported a year earlier.
Verizon 2025 Data Breach Investigations Report (global, 12,195 breaches)
93%
of Indian financial institutions surveyed by RBI depend partially or substantially on third parties for core cybersecurity work such as SOC monitoring, cloud security and threat intelligence.
RBI Financial Stability Report, June 2026
6 hours
to report a cyber incident to RBI through the DAKSH platform, counted from detection, with CERT-In notified as well. A vendor's late notice eats into that window.
RBI Cybersecurity, Technology Risk, Resilience and Assurance Directions, 31 July 2026

RBI's outsourcing Directions for commercial banks (28 November 2025) also require a central inventory of outsourced IT services, mapping of dependencies on third parties and their key subcontractors, and contracts that give the bank and RBI audit rights and timely incident notification.

The gap Fortivance closes

Most vendor risk tools answer "is this vendor risky?". During an incident, the questions a bank actually has to answer are different.

How Fortivance uses AI responsibly

  • People decideEvery AI output is marked as needing review. Response plans need an approver; evidence findings need an analyst to verify or dispute them.
  • No regulatory verdictsFortivance shows the reporting window but never decides whether an incident is reportable and never files a report.
  • Citations that can be checkedThe AI may cite only clause identifiers from the document. Fortivance checks that each one exists and shows the original text; anything it cannot verify is sent back for review.
  • Untrusted input stays untrustedUploaded notices are treated as data. Instructions hidden inside them are flagged and ignored, and a notice from an unrecognised domain gets no automatic plan.
  • Explainable risk scoresVendor risk scores are rule-based. Each contributing factor and its points are listed.
  • Controlled costResults are cached, every AI call is logged with its token usage, and a daily spending cap is enforced.
  • Measured against gold dataAI outputs for incident extraction, evidence mapping and citations are continuously measured against hand-labelled data on the Evaluation page.
