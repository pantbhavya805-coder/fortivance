How the score works

The score shows how much risk is left after counting what the bank can prove is under control (residual risk), on a scale of 0 to 100.

  • Starting point (inherent risk): how critical the vendor is (CRITICAL 40, HIGH 30, MEDIUM 20, LOW 10) and what data it handles (financial 15, customer personal data 10, internal 5).
  • Missing or partial evidence (+8 / +4 per requirement): a control the bank cannot prove exists gives no protection, so the risk stays.
  • Open incidents in the vendor's chain (+5 each): something is going wrong right now. These points drop off when the incident is closed.
  • Overdue periodic review (+10) and subcontractors with no assurance report on file (+5 each): blind spots in oversight and in the supply chain.

Bands: 70 and above HIGH, 40 to 69 MEDIUM, below 40 LOW. The checks follow the RBI Outsourcing Directions 2025; the point weights are the bank's own settings, not an RBI formula. No AI is used in the score.
