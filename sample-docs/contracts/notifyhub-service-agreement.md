# SERVICE AGREEMENT — MESSAGING AND NOTIFICATION SERVICES

**Reference:** NH/ENT/2024/0387
**Between:** NotifyHub Communications Pvt Ltd ("NotifyHub"), Pune
**And:** Deccan Meridian Bank Ltd ("Customer"), Hyderabad

## 1. Services
1.1 NotifyHub will provide SMS, email and push-notification delivery services, including one-time password (OTP) delivery, transaction alerts and marketing messages, through its messaging platform and API.
1.2 NotifyHub may update, modify or improve the platform from time to time.

## 2. Service Levels
2.1 NotifyHub will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the platform available 99.5% of the time each month, excluding scheduled maintenance and events outside NotifyHub's reasonable control, including failures of telecom operators or aggregators.
2.2 OTP messages will be submitted to the carrier network within 5 seconds under normal operating conditions.

## 3. Customer Data
3.1 NotifyHub will process mobile numbers, email addresses and message content supplied by the Customer solely to provide the Services.
3.2 NotifyHub will retain message logs in accordance with its standard data retention policy.
3.3 NotifyHub will implement reasonable technical and organisational security measures.

## 4. Security Incidents
4.1 NotifyHub will inform the Customer of any security incident affecting Customer Data **promptly upon becoming aware of it, as reasonably practicable**.

## 5. Use of Third Parties
5.1 NotifyHub may engage subcontractors, telecom aggregators and other third-party service providers at its discretion to deliver the Services.
5.2 NotifyHub remains responsible for its obligations under this Agreement.

## 6. Information Requests
6.1 On written request, not more than once per year, NotifyHub will provide the Customer with a summary of its security practices.

## 7. Business Continuity
7.1 NotifyHub maintains business continuity arrangements consistent with industry practice.

## 8. Fees
8.1 Fees are as per the Commercial Annexure. NotifyHub may revise fees on 30 days' notice.

## 9. Term and Termination
9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 30 days' written notice.
9.2 On termination, the Customer's access to the platform will cease on the termination date.

## 10. Limitation of Liability
10.1 NotifyHub's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the 3 months preceding the claim.
