**Classification:** Unauthorised access — production payment switch
**Detected:** 20 November 2026, 17:50 IST, by our security operations centre

**Summary:** Our monitoring detected repeated logins to the production card authorisation and UPI switching environment using a valid administrator account from an unrecognised network. The account was disabled at 18:05 IST. While the affected switching nodes were isolated for investigation, a portion of UPI and card transactions for client banks, including Deccan Meridian Bank, failed or timed out.

**Production status:** Production is **affected**. Around 30% of UPI and card authorisation requests are failing. We are restoring service on clean nodes and expect partial recovery within 2 to 3 hours.

**Data:** We have not yet determined whether transaction data or cardholder information was accessed. Forensic investigation has started with an external incident response firm.

**Actions requested of Deccan Meridian Bank:**
1. Monitor UPI and card decline rates and inform us of any unusual transaction patterns.
2. Rotate the API keys and certificates used for the PayRail production integration once we confirm the new endpoints.
